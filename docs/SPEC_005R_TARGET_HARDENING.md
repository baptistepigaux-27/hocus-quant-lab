# SPEC-005R — Target coverage and extreme-return hardening

SPEC-005R adds auditability to the V1 target factory. It does not add a model,
portfolio, backtest, or target transformation. Raw target values remain intact;
in particular, no winsorization or return clipping is performed.

## Availability semantics

`right_censored_end_of_sample` means the available corpus ends before the
requested Hth future session. It is reported separately from
`insufficient_future_history`, which means the corpus contains at least H
future sessions globally but this entity's own history does not. Both remain
in the raw target table and are unavailable for research use.

Coverage reports include the raw available/candidate ratio and an additional
ratio with right-censored candidates removed from the denominator. They are
broken down by cutoff, target family, horizon, and benchmark mapping. Relative
targets distinguish `benchmark_not_defined` from a defined mapping whose
benchmark history is unavailable or incomplete.

## Extreme-return review

Available or candidate absolute and relative returns with absolute magnitude
at least 90% are reviewed against the underlying asset and benchmark price
paths. The audit records the path, large adjacent price transitions, source
series, and diagnostic flags. Events are grouped by entity and their largest
material transition so the same suspected corporate action is not counted
once per horizon. Classifications are heuristic diagnostics, not confirmed
corporate-action labels.

The raw Parquet targets retain every computed candidate. A separate
`targets_research_ready` view and per-slice
`targets_research_ready.parquet` include only available targets, excluding
right-censored and future-quality review/quarantine rows. Extreme rows are
excluded only when their event remains in an unresolved source, scale,
benchmark, corporate-action, discontinuity, or insufficient-evidence class.
Plausible market moves are retained and flagged for audit.

## Cohorts and execution grid

The aggregate audit reports the first and last cutoff with usable observations
for each horizon and the per-cutoff candidate, available, censoring, quality,
and benchmark counts. The set builder consumes the sparse cutoff grid from the
feature-cube manifests. A weekly grid can be prepared from that manifest grid;
the audit also computes the latest theoretically observable Friday cutoff for
each horizon from the source corpus boundary. These theoretical limits are
separate from last usable cutoffs actually materialized in the sparse run. The
full daily entity × cutoff × target cube is intentionally not generated by this
SPEC.

## Artifacts and limits

Each target-set output contains `target_hardening_audit.json` and
`target_hardening_audit.md`; local data artifacts are not checked into Git.
Availability describes this reconstructed historical corpus and does not
claim strict historical point-in-time capture. The extreme-event classifier
uses price-path evidence and simple transition heuristics; ambiguous cases are
kept out of the research-ready view pending review, without modifying raw
values.
